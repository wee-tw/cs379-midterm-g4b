# Draft GitHub issue for Xilinx/finn (not posted; needs the user's approval)

**Title:** Wide-SIMD MVUs miss 100 MHz on Zynq-7000 (v0.10.1 RTL lane adder; folded MVAU_hls), and SetFolding has no timing guard

## Summary
FINN's resource estimates and `SetFolding` treat SIMD as free in timing. On xc7z020 (PYNQ-Z2, for which v0.10.1 is the last supported release), the post-synthesis critical path of the MVU grows with SIMD. Wide foldings miss 125 MHz and often 100 MHz:
- `MVAU_rtl`: about 1.2 ns per doubling of SIMD.
- `MVAU_hls` with input folding (SF = MW / SIMD > 1).

The default `mvau_wwidth_max = 36` keeps designs in the safe range. Raising it to reach a throughput target, or folding by hand, does not. (`SetFolding` checks the cycle target before the width, so the last SIMD step may exceed the cap: 2-bit weights reach SIMD 24, i.e. 48 bits. Such units still met 10 ns in our runs.)

## Evidence
Vivado / Vitis HLS 2022.2, `xc7z020clg400-1`, out of context, post-synthesis. All nodes were generated by FINN v0.10.1 from a ResNet-8 at 2/4/8-bit widths.
- **`MVAU_rtl`, 53 units, clock given to synthesis:**

  | SIMD | Period (ns) |
  |---|---|
  | ≤ 12 | 3.8–6.3 |
  | 16 | 7.3–7.8 |
  | 24–27 | 7.7–8.3 |
  | 48 | 9.7–10.3 |
  | 72–96 | 10.4–11.9 |
  | 144–288 | 11.8–12.6 |

  - The critical path runs from a DSP output through the lane-correction adder tree (`genLo` / `Lo4` in `mvu_4sx4u.sv` and `mvu_8sx8u_dsp48.sv`). It is summed combinationally over SIMD and registered once: 16 logic levels at SIMD 288.
  - PE hardly matters: PE 8, SIMD 18 synthesizes to 8.19 ns at a 9 ns clock.
- **`MVAU_hls`, 331 runs (`export_design -flow syn`):**
  - SF = 1 stayed at or below 7.63 ns at every SIMD up to 288.
  - SF > 1 with SIMD ≤ 27 met 10 ns in all 207 runs (worst 8.80 ns), but 8 of 9 missed 8 ns.
  - SF > 1 with SIMD ≥ 48 missed 10 ns in 21 of 36 runs and 8 ns in 18 of 18 (worst 13.1 ns).
- **Post-route:** out-of-context place and route of single units, with the steps of Vivado's default implementation strategy (`opt_design`, `place_design`, `phys_opt_design`, `route_design`), an 8 ns clock and the ports as partition pins.
  - `MVAU_rtl` at SIMD 48 routes to 9.85 ns (4-bit core) and 10.90 ns (8-bit core).
  - At PE 8–16 and SIMD 18 it routes to 8.18–8.58 ns, missing 125 MHz; the critical path stays DSP output → `Lo4`.
  - The same layers refolded to SIMD 9–12 with PE up to 32 (same lanes) route to 7.00–7.93 ns.
  - With many input folds and large PE the control path becomes the limit: MW 576, PE 16, SIMD 12 routes to 8.13 ns. At PE 32 / SIMD 6 it routes to 7.99 ns and at PE 64 / SIMD 3 to 7.45 ns (same 48 DSPs). The critical path is then the fan-out of `A_reg[rdy]` to the clock enables of the DSPs and data registers, with no logic levels.
  - Folded `MVAU_hls`: 8.19–8.22 ns at SIMD 18; 9.45 ns at SIMD 24 (PE 8, 2-bit LUT products), which misses 111 MHz.
  - `MVAU_rtl` at SIMD 72 (PE 4, 8-bit) and SIMD 144 (PE 2, 4-bit) route to 12.57 and 13.67 ns, 19% and 15% above their post-synthesis periods.
  - In 25 of 28 routed units, the post-route clock class (FCLK0 = 1000/n MHz) matches the post-synthesis one. The three exceptions are the SIMD-24 `MVAU_hls` and the SIMD-72 and SIMD-144 `MVAU_rtl`, each one class slower.
- **dev fixes the RTL part:** `mvu.sv` sums the lanes with `add_multi`, pipelined to `$clog2(SIMD)` stages.
  - We instantiated dev's `mvu_vvu_axi_wrapper.v` template with the generics of four of the v0.10.1 units above (`VERSION = 1`) and routed them the same way.
  - They route to 4.87–6.81 ns, where v0.10.1 needs 8.44–13.67 ns (SIMD 18–144). The critical path becomes replay-buffer BRAM → DSP input.
  - The DSP count is unchanged; LUTs grow by 10–45%.

## Suggestion
- Document a timing-safe SIMD bound for 7-series parts, or let `SetFolding` cap SIMD for folded `MVAU_hls` independently of `mvau_wwidth_max`. On xc7z020 after routing, SIMD ≤ 12 kept `MVAU_rtl` at 125 MHz in 11 of 12 routed units (PE up to 32; the exception, MW 576 at PE 16 / SIMD 12, needed PE 64 / SIMD 3), and SIMD ≤ 18 kept folded `MVAU_hls` at 111 MHz. Replicating the `rdy` enable per DSP group would help at large PE.
- Prefer SF = 1, or more PE, when a throughput target needs more lanes.
- Backporting the pipelined lane adder would help users who are pinned to v0.10.1 for PYNQ-Z1/Z2. Until then, narrowing SIMD and raising PE keeps the same lanes and DSP count and reaches 125 MHz after routing (SIMD 9–12, PE up to 32: 7.00–7.93 ns).

## Not verified
- Post-route timing of complete designs: we routed single units out of context, which is a lower bound on the period inside a full design.
- UltraScale+ parts.
